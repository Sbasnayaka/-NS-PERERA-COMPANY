FIRM’S PROFILE

Trusted Auditors & Tax Consultants for Business Success

NS Perera Company is an independent body of Accountants & Auditors registered as an auditor under the Companies (Auditors) Registration 1964 with the Registrar of Companies.

Our work is strictly based on professionalism, integrity, and specialized knowledge. Our corporate philosophy is to offer high-quality added value services to our clients that will serve their economic interests in the best possible way. All engagements are carried out using standard international methodology which ensures excellence and strict risk control procedures through a deep due diligence analysis.

We treat all business data with the absolute strictest professional confidence, maintaining the highest ethical and moral standards. Our ultimate goal is to provide innovative, compliant solutions to each client in order to succeed in their challenges.

EY Big-4 Executive Background
